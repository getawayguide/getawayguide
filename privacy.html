Privacy policy

Information we collect

Getawayguide collects only the information you choose to provide. This includes your email address if you subscribe to the newsletter, and any details you submit via the contact form. We do not collect any information passively or without your knowledge.

How we use your information

Your email address is used solely to send you newsletter updates from Getawayguide. We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. We do not build user profiles or share data with advertising networks.

Affiliate links

Some of the links in my guides and on the Resources page are affiliate links, mainly to booking sites like Hostelworld, GetYourGuide, Viator and Booking.com. If you book through one of them I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only link to places I actually stayed at, ate at or booked myself, and earning a commission never changes what I recommend or how I write about it.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. To submit a request, please use the contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

Tools & recommendations

Travel resources

Everything I actually use and trust from the gear I've carried across 60 countries to websites and apps I use to plan every single trip

Websites, Apps & Gear

The websites, apps and physical gear I carry on every trip

✈

Getting There

Useful tools for planning your flights and buses

Google Flights

The main flight aggregator I use. Set up email alerts to track specific flights or routes
Priority Pass

Lounges are a lifesaver when traveling. I'm not sure I'd pay for this on my own, but it comes free with many premium credit cards
Traveling

One of many aggregators for booking buses and trains in Europe. Depending on the region, I would use a similar website for planning routes

🗺

How I Plan

Helpful tools for planning your itinerary and figuring out logistics & transport

Rome2Rio

Perhaps the #1 tool for planning trips. Shows every possible route by plane, bus, car and ferry between two points
Stippl

One of the best route-planning tools out there. Plan your trip day by day and easily reorder or add new locations
Notion

A simple note-taking app I use for trip notes and itineraries

🏨

Booking Platforms

Where I book my next night on the trip

HostelWorld

The best hostel booking platform. The chat feature is useful for meeting other backpackers
Expedia

The premier booking aggregator. I mainly use it for the loyalty program and cash back
Airbnb

Often cheaper than hotels. I book mostly Airbnbs when I'm working remote
Booking.com

The most comprehensive booking aggregator, with a lot of properties you won't find elsewhere

📱

Apps I Use

Helpful apps that I use on almost every trip. Don't overcomplicate it!

Google Maps

Download the offline maps and save every location you plan to visit before you land
AllTrails

Great for planning hikes. The offline maps help you stay on track when you're out on the trails
Airalo

eSIMs are a great way to stay connected. I always download one before landing in a new country
Charles Schwab

The investor checking account has a debit card with no ATM fees, making getting cash simple anywhere

🎟

Tours & Day Trips

For the days I'd rather not plan logistics myself

GetYourGuide

A reliable site for booking day tours. I read reviews carefully and compare options before booking
Viator

Another solid tour booking app, with many operators and extensive reviews

🎒

Gear I Carry

I also invest a lot of time finding the right gear for travel. Here are some of my favorites

Bellroy Transit Travel Pack Pro

My main bag for the last 3 trips. I like to travel light, and it expands from 30L to 38L while still fitting under the airplane seat
Peak Design Packing Cubes

Packing cubes are key for staying organized while backpacking. I like the compression and durability of these
Heimplanet Carry Essentials Dopp Kit

A reliable waterproof bag for my toiletries

Helpful blogs & videos

The independent writers and creators I actually turn to before a trip

Reading

Favorite blogs

I spend a lot of time on other travel blogs while researching my next trip. Here are some of my favorites

Sallysees Central America

The best blog on Central America. I used it extensively for past trips to Guatemala, Nicaragua, El Salvador and Mexico
Wander Lush Caucasus & Balkans

One of my favorite travel blogs, with extremely detailed guides on the Caucasus and the Balkans
BucketListly All-around guide

A great all-around blog with guides on almost every country you can think of
Johnny Africa All-Around Guide

Detailed articles on countries, FIRE and travel blogging. A bit dated, but I used it to plan my recent trip to Greece

Watching

Favorite YouTube

Where I go for inspiration and country-by-country planning

Backpacking Bananas

One of my favorite backpacking channels, with great country guide playlists. I used her channel to plan my trips to Australia and Central America
Ryan Shirley

Home to some of the most incredible travel drone footage on YouTube, and thorough country guides to match
World Wild Hearts

A couple making well-thought-out travel guides. I use their tips on almost every trip, including Türkiye, Albania and Portugal
Tales from the Road

A smaller channel with travel guides on lesser-visited countries, including Central Asia and North Africa

Option A — Light Editorial

☀️
When to Go
Nov–Apr dry season. May–Oct brings lush landscapes and fewer tourists.
📅
How Long
1 week covers the highlights. 2 weeks lets you slow down and see everything.
💵
Daily Budget
$30–45 budget; $60–90 mid-range. USD used everywhere — no exchange needed.
✈️
Getting There
Fly into SAL (San Salvador International). Well connected from the US.
🚌
Getting Around
Chicken buses $0.50–2. Uber in cities. Car rental for flexibility.
🛂
Visa
Visa-free 90 days for US, EU, UK, AU. Shared CA-4 quota with Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua.
🛡️
Safety
Now one of the safest countries in the Western Hemisphere following the 2022 crackdown.
💰
Currency
US Dollar (USD). Bitcoin is also legal tender. ATMs widely available.

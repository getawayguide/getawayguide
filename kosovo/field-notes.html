Kosovo

Places to visit

General

  • One of the most pro-American places you'll ever visit — US flags everywhere, streets named after Bill Clinton and Bob Dole. Being American here gets you a genuinely warm reception.
  • Kosovo declared independence in 2008 and is still not recognized by everyone (notably Serbia). Safe for tourists — KFOR NATO peacekeeping troops are still stationed here
  • Super cheap — burek and a drink under 3 euros, coffee for 2 euros, fancy dinners for almost nothing
  • Limited time needed: Prishtina is a half day, Prizren deserves a full day or two

Prishtina

Half day recommended
What to Do
  • Bill Clinton Statue — full-size statue on Bill Klinton Boulevard next to a huge poster of him on a communist-era building. There's also a bust of Bob Dole nearby. Peak Kosovo
  • Mother Teresa Church Bell Tower — one of the few places where saying you're American gets a genuinely warm reaction. Great view from the top
  • Kosovo National Library — wild brutalist building with boxy shapes, white hexagonal domes and metal netting on the exterior. Inside there's an America Corner with Bill Clinton's biography and a merged US/Kosovo flag
  • Bunkart 2 — converted Cold War bunker museum. Very well done

Prizren

1–2 days recommended
  • The real reason to come to Kosovo. Small Ottoman old town, a fortress with incredible views and a genuinely great vibe at night.
What to Do
  • Fortress at Sunset — steep walk up a cobblestone hill but the view from the top is genuinely insane. Go at sunset, bring a beer
  • League of Prizren — where Albanian independence was first organized. Small complex with traditional Albanian outfits and nationalist paintings
  • Main Mosque — has Art Deco-style painted walls inside and a Star of David. Unusual compared to other mosques in the region
  • Serbian Orthodox Church — attacked during riots in 2023 and now guarded by police 24/7. Worth seeing to understand the tensions
  • Free walking tour — the guide is usually a local university student and very candid. The political layer here is unlike anywhere else in the Balkans
  • Craft Brewery — great spot to sit outside with locals at night
  • Archaeological Museum — old prehistoric, Roman and Ottoman artifacts. Also has a clock tower you can climb
Where to Eat
  • Steakhouse Sarajeva — fancier spot by the water but still incredibly cheap. Get the Balkan burger
  • Te Syla — by the river. Get the Qebap (their name for Cevapi)
  • Napoletana Nostra — nice Italian restaurant
  • Gatsby — popular coffee spot among locals serving Western food

Other Places to Consider

  • Peja — a town with great nature and hiking located near Montenegro (more detailed guide here)
  • Gjakova – a small town close to the Albania border with an old bazaar and traditional Ottoman architecture (more detailed guide here)

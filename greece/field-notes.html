Greece

Places to visit

General

Greeks are genuinely friendly, especially if you use basic greetings. Kalimera (good morning) and Kalispera (good evening) instead of hello gets a great reaction every time. Bus system between cities is reliable and easy, though paper tickets are required at some stations. Food is very affordable at local spots.

Thessaloniki

1–2 days recommended

More of a real city than a tourist one, which is refreshing after the Balkans. Worth a stop if you're crossing between Greece and North Macedonia.

What to Do
  • White Tower Museum — the symbol of the city and the best museum here. Each floor covers a different aspect of Thessaloniki's history — Byzantine, Ottoman, Jewish and modern. Get the English audio guide
  • Kapani Market — very authentically Greek. Goat heads, fresh fish, produce. Not touristy at all
  • Platia Aristotelous — the main walking street leading down to the waterfront
  • Arch of Galerius — really well-preserved Roman arch with detailed marble carvings
  • Hagia Sophia & Holy Church of St. Demetrius — both beautiful Byzantine churches with gold mosaics
Where to Eat
  • Τα μικρά Καλύβια — local homestyle place, cafeteria format. Meatballs in tomato sauce and rice. 4.5€. Unreal value

Meteora / Kastraki

2–3 days recommended

One of the most impressive landscapes I've ever seen — monasteries perched on top of towering rock pillars rising out of a green valley. Do not skip this.

Where to Stay
  • Stay in Kastraki over Kalambaka — the small village right at the base of the rocks. Much more peaceful, better views and everything is 5 minutes away
What to Do
  • Rent an e-bike — the best way to get around. The downhills are insanely fun. 15–20€ for the day
  • Sunset viewpoint — go for sunset, not midday. Tour buses clear out and the light on the monasteries is incredible
  • Great Meteoron — the biggest monastery with a wine cellar, museum and old church
  • Roussanou — has a really beautiful small prayer room inside
  • Varlaam — the most castle-like and impressive from the outside. Check opening days — each monastery closes on a different day of the week
  • Holy Spirit Rock hike — not on Google Maps or AllTrails. 40-minute hike straight up to a tiny white church embedded in the rock with a ladder to the very top. Best view of Kastraki and you'll probably have it entirely to yourself
  • Hiking between monasteries — get a paper map from your accommodation
Where to Eat
  • Taverna Gardenia (Kastraki) — get the local monastic beer and the moussaka
  • Restaurant Meteora Gkertsou Family (Kalambaka) — lamb cooked in red wine with rice. Huge portion

Athens

2–3 days recommended

Athens is one of those cities where the history is everywhere — ancient ruins sitting in the middle of neighborhoods, columns poking out from under metro stations. Grittier than you might expect but worth the time.

What to Do
  • Acropolis — the must-do. Combined ticket gets you into 5 other sites. The hill is pretty big so give it a few hours. Go early — it gets brutally hot and crowded by midday
  • Ancient Agora — super well preserved temple complex at the base of the Acropolis
  • Hadrian's Library — I'd skip this one
  • Plaka neighborhood — the nicest neighborhood in Athens. Winding streets, neoclassical buildings and lots of good spots to eat
  • Syntagma Square — changing of the guard every day at 11am in front of the Parliament building
  • Monastiraki Square — lively square near the flea market with a nice view of the Acropolis
Where to Eat
  • Bairaktaris — Aiolou 29. Classic Greek spot right by Monastiraki

Santorini

3–4 days recommended

The most iconic island in Greece and fully lives up to the hype visually. Gets extremely crowded in peak season — book accommodation well in advance.

What to Do
  • Hike from Fira to Oia — 2–3 hours, about 7 miles. Pretty well labeled trail along the caldera rim with incredible views the whole way
  • Watch the sunset at Oia — the famous one. Gets absolutely packed — arrive an hour early
  • Ride a donkey up the steps at Fira — touristy but kind of fun
  • Perissa Beach — the black sand beach on the south side of the island
  • Boat tour around the caldera — goes around the caldera and hot springs. The fancy version includes dinner, unlimited drinks and sunset views
  • Rent an ATV or moped — the best way to get around the island
Where to Eat
  • Svoronos Bakery — amazing bakery in Thira
  • Taverna El Greco — decent brunch spot

Mykonos

2–3 days recommended

The party island of Greece. Beautiful town, great beaches and genuinely fun nightlife — but very expensive and very crowded in summer.

What to Do
  • Windmills — the iconic symbol of the island. Great at sunset
  • Little Venice — bars and restaurants built right over the water. Some of the nicest spots to have a drink with a view
  • Tropicana — beach club. The classic Mykonos beach party experience
  • Cavo Paradiso — nightclub right next to Tropicana. One of the most famous clubs in Greece
Where to Eat
  • Niko's Tavern — solid, reliable Greek spot
  • Souvlaki Story — great place for gyros
  • Attica Bakery — great bakery for breakfast

Naxos

2–3 days recommended

The most underrated of the Cyclades. Bigger than the other islands, more local feeling and with better food than anywhere else I went in Greece. Don't skip this one.

What to Do
  • Temple of Apollo (Portara) — a massive ancient marble doorway standing alone on a peninsula near the port. One of the coolest things I saw in Greece. Great at sunset
  • Old Market Street — the main shopping street through the old town
  • Venetian Castle — castle at the top of the old town with good views
  • Agios Georgios Beach — the main town beach, easy walk from the port
  • Boat tour to Koufonisia — I didn't get to do this because of weather, but this island is supposed to have the most beautiful beaches in all of Greece
Where to Eat
  • Menu με νου — my favorite restaurant in Greece. Really good lamb chops. Don't miss this

Ios

2–3 days recommended

Ios has a reputation as a party island but it's a lot more than that — beautiful beaches, incredible sunsets and a surprisingly charming old town on the hill.

Where to Stay
  • Francesco's Hostel — super social with a pool and a nice view over the water. The go-to hostel on the island
What to Do
  • Pathos Bar — amazing day bar with modern art installations and my favorite place to watch a sunset on the entire trip. Don't miss this
  • ATV around the island — 35€ per day. The best way to explore
  • Paralia Gialos — the main beach near the port
  • Paralia Milopotas — nice beach near Far Out Beach Club
  • Magganari Beach — considered the nicest beach on the island. Can't get there by ATV — take the boat taxi
  • Loretzaina — really small, rarely visited private beach
  • Hidden cliff viewpoint — the most beautiful spot on the island to look at the water. Not well marked — explore on your ATV or ask locals
  • Agios Ioannis — remote church with a cool courtyard
Where to Eat & Drink
  • Kabouri's Restaurant — really good Greek food. Nicer sit-down spot
  • Beach House Ios — right on a beach. Really good shrimp
  • Shot Bar — cheap with fun fire shots
  • Escobar Ios — fun bar
  • Shush Silent Disco — really fun silent disco
  • Lost Boys — more trendy bar

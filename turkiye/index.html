Türkiye

One of my favorite countries in the world, Türkiye serves as the intersection between east and west. The country hosts a wide range of different attractions from impressive mosques in Istanbul, the world-class beaches of the Turkish Riviera, the magical rock formations in Cappadocia and an amazing cuisine.
